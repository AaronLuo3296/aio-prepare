# Day 30: Retrospective and roadmap

**Goal:** measure the 30 days honestly and leave with a concrete plan for the next months.
**Math bridge:** as after a training camp, the useful output is a list of what to work
on next, not a feeling about how it went.

---

## 1. Measure

Fill in the progress tracker at the end of the [plan](../plan/30-day-plan.md):

- Mock scores #1–#4. Is the trend up? Did the first two Senior problems reach full marks?
- CSES problems solved (target: about 80).
- Checkpoints passed for every day in Weeks 1–4. List the ones you have not passed. They
  are the first items of next week's plan.

## 3. What comes next

The full plan is in [after-30-days.md](../plan/after-30-days.md). In short, the IOI-track
topics come roughly in this order:

1. Strings: hashing, KMP / Z-function (CPH ch. 26).
2. Strongly connected components, 2-SAT (CPH ch. 17).
3. Lazy propagation and advanced segment trees (CPH ch. 28).
4. Square-root techniques (CPH ch. 27).
5. Flows (CPH ch. 20), then geometry and sweep line (CPH ch. 29–30).
6. Advanced DP optimisations (USACO Guide Platinum).

**Weekly habits:** one rated contest ([Codeforces](https://codeforces.com/) Div. 2/3 or
AtCoder ABC/ARC) followed by upsolving, IOI problems on oj.uz worked through by year, and
the remaining CSES sections.

**Pathway:** AIO → AIIO (invitational) → Australian training school / team selection →
IOI. Check the [Australian Maths Trust](https://www.amt.edu.au/) for the current year's
dates and rules.

**Checkpoint:** the progress tracker filled in, the error-log table complete, and a
written plan for the next four weeks that starts with your unpassed checkpoints.
