# Day 29: Mock contest #4

**Goal:** one more full contest, to check that the Day 28 consolidation stuck and that the
routine holds under pressure.
**Math bridge:** two data points (mocks #3 and #4) a day apart show your current level
better than one.

---

## 1. Choose a format

- **Option A:** another unseen Senior paper from [ORAC AIO Sets](https://orac2.info/hub/aio/),
  under the same conditions as Day 27.
- **Option B:** an [AtCoder](https://atcoder.jp/) Beginner Contest, live or as a virtual
  contest, problems A–E. The problems are short and precisely stated, and the ranking
  gives feedback right away. Use this if you have run out of unseen AIO papers.

## 2. One deliberate focus

Choose **one** habit your error log says you skip, and do it on every problem today.
Examples: "write N's bound and the target complexity before coding", "stress-test before
submitting anything non-trivial", "re-read the output format".

## 3. After the contest

1. Record the score in the progress tracker.
2. Upsolve. On AtCoder, read the editorial for the first problem you could not solve, and
   implement it the same day.
3. Did the focus habit cost or save time? Write one sentence about it.

**Checkpoint:** mock #4 upsolved, its score recorded, and the focus habit evaluated.
